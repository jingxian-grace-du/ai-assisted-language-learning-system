# Intended learners and boundaries

## Intended learners

The system is primarily for people who:

- already possess a functional foundation in English;
- can communicate basic meaning but struggle to speak naturally or consistently;
- do not know how to integrate English into everyday life;
- value reflection, journaling, or deliberate self-observation;
- are willing to practise over a long period;
- want personally relevant material rather than a generic syllabus;
- are interested in using AI as both a language tool and a structured thinking partner.

An existing journaling habit may make adoption easier, but it is not a requirement.

## Not the primary audience

The system is not designed as the main solution for learners who:

- still need a basic vocabulary and grammar curriculum;
- require urgent preparation for a specific examination;
- expect guaranteed improvement within a short period;
- want passive content consumption without regular spoken output;
- are unwilling to review selected material over time.

These boundaries do not judge those goals. They prevent the project from claiming to solve a different learning problem.

## Readiness for Stage 2

Stage 2 should not begin merely because the software is available. Useful indicators include:

- the learner can sustain recurring English oral-diary sessions;
- sessions contain genuine attempts to express lived meaning;
- the learner increasingly describes unknown concepts in English;
- enough material accumulates to reveal reusable expressions and reasoning patterns;
- notebook generation and Anki review will support, rather than overwhelm, the oral habit.

Readiness should eventually be operationalised as guidance, not enforced as a rigid universal streak or duration.

## Claims the project must avoid

The project must not equate pipeline accuracy with language improvement. A target such as 95% coverage or precision describes processing quality against an approved reference; it does not mean a learner's English will improve by 95%.

The project also must not promise therapeutic, psychological, educational, or life outcomes from AI conversation.
