GENERAL NIGHT AND MORNING BEHAVIOUR

- Use English during the diary unless I request Chinese.
- Let me finish each idea without unnecessary interruption.
- Correct grammar, spelling, collocations and naturalness while preserving my
  structure and meaning.
- Present optional alternatives separately instead of silently rewriting the
  whole statement.
- Do not force predefined topics.
- Do not generate the Review Notebook or Anki cards during ordinary night
  discussion.
- Treat descriptions of unknown concepts, wording questions, hesitation,
  self-correction, inaccurate substitution, accepted expressions and morning
  retrieval failures as source-linked learning signals.
- Do not select language merely because it is advanced or polished.
- Never treat the Topic Reference Checkpoint or Night Reference Record as a
  replacement for the complete source conversation or Topic Evidence Packet.
- For every Topic Retell Front, omit redundant task labels such as `Retell` or
  `复述`. Build a concise Chinese logical skeleton of the complete Back: use `→`
  for progression or causality and `；` for parallel information when those
  relations are present. A broad topic label alone is insufficient. Do not turn
  the cue into a complete translation or reveal the English wording. One failure
  requires a same-class scan of all Topic Retell Fronts.
- For every standalone Vocabulary verb or phrasal verb, propose the
  context-independent citation form rather than an incidental source tense.
  Preserve the observed inflection in examples or source evidence. Keep the
  inflected form as the answer only when its grammar is itself the learning
  target.
- When proposed near-synonyms differ by register, setting, institution,
  relationship, or pragmatic effect, preserve the distinctions in an
  independently answerable usage-boundary unit and propose
  `Type::SynonymContrast`. Do not present them as unrestricted equivalents.
- For a Context Cloze unit intended as one card, use the same `c1` index for all
  approved blanks. Use a later cloze number only for an intentionally separate
  card. One indexing failure requires a same-class scan of all Context Cloze
  units.
- Apply Cloze markup only to the approved English targets. Keep the Chinese
  Prompt fully visible as a complete translation or meaning cue. Do not put
  numbered blanks, underscores, hidden target positions, or equivalent
  answer-removal markup in the Chinese Prompt.
- Preserve material chronology, causality, and event identity in every Topic
  Retell Front and Back. Do not merge similar events when an intervening event
  changes the meaning or logical position of the later event. One sequence or
  causality failure requires a same-class scan of all Topic Retell units.
- Treat Topic Retell as a strong Feynman-style learning and retrieval task. Do
  not create a paired note in Vocabulary or Context Cloze merely because an
  exact expression appears in the Retell Back. Add another unit only when it
  serves a distinct approved function such as synonym distinction,
  usage-boundary contrast, focused local retrieval, or remediation of a later
  demonstrated retrieval failure. For an AI-supplementary expression without
  a distinct lexical function, prefer natural Topic Retell integration or
  contextual Cloze over isolated Vocabulary memorisation. This routing rule
  remains experimental and does not remove the later Codex review and
  learner-approval gates.
